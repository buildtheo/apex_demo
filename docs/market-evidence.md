## Implications for Apex

- Market evidence supports a meaningful operational problem. It does not establish demand for our solution or prove its economics.
- The $20 baseline is the assumed 20 minutes x $60/hour for evidence-intensive investigations, not an asserted portfolio average. The $9.08-$10.32 published average does not verify that tail.
- Our 60,000 eligible cases are also an assumed 50% of 120,000 total cases. Calling them a subset is insufficient: at $20 each, they already contribute $10 per total portfolio dispute before the other half's costs. Different populations or methods could explain the difference; the case mix and costs must be measured, not explained away.
- Validate eligible counts, complexity bands, all-role handling time, follow-up/rework, and cost definitions. Do not imply the single unrecognized-purchase hero case proves applicability across the whole eligible class.
- Do not inflate volume or introduce speculative fines, recovery, churn, or loss avoidance to rescue a weak ROI.
- Retain the growth-capacity hypothesis and downside model in business-case.md. The 18,687.5-hour calculation is valid arithmetic under assumptions, not a measured outcome or headcount commitment.

## Executive framing

Dispute volumes are substantial and growing. For Apex, we propose testing whether better intake and evidence investigation can absorb growth without proportional staffing increases. The pilot establishes eligible workload, actual effort saved, decision quality, and net cost.

Discovery: What is your annual dispute volume, how much is genuinely evidence-intensive, what handling effort occurs across roles, and what network/case-management tools already support it?

## Competitive position

OpenAI is a proposed way to deliver the bank's customer conversation and analyst investigation over its governed tools. Existing network intelligence, evidence services, and the DMS may remain valuable inputs or alternatives. Compare incremental outcomes and total cost; do not position OpenAI merely as a PDF summarizer or claim it outperforms Visa's tools without testing.

No interview upload, proprietary bank policy, live customer data, or confidential network report is committed here.
