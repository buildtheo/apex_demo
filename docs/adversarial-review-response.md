## Required investigation brief

The handoff should contain:
- Case ID, transaction reference, evidence snapshot, and source IDs.
- Customer statement attributed as a statement, not assumed fact.
- Supported findings with source references and a distinction between assertion, tool observation, and inference.
- Conflicting evidence, material limitations, and unanswered questions.
- Requested specialist decision and why this role is appropriate.
- Exact destination queue, proposed action, and explanation for analyst review.

Keep expected answers in the evaluator's key, outside model-visible evidence. A brief is a model-produced work product subject to analyst review, not a new authoritative source for its own claims.

Approval binds the case, snapshot, exact action payload and brief, recipient, actor, and expiration. Changed evidence or payload requires renewed review. An acknowledgement alone is insufficient: read back the expected request or assignment and stored brief (or its exact version/hash), not just a matching case status. Preserve duplicate and failure reconciliation controls.
