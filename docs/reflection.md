## What I would do earlier

Start with a day alongside the analysts: real volumes, active handling times, representative cases, qualitative judgment, workarounds, existing automation and constraints. Observe intake through resolution and downstream review/rework, separating hands-on effort from external waiting.

In a real implementation this precedes final scope, benefit estimates and solution design. No Apex ride-along or measured baseline has occurred. Existing numbers are transparent scenario assumptions.

## Why an executive art-of-the-possible demo is still worth doing

A concrete demonstration can help C-level leaders see an opportunity and articulate priorities they would otherwise struggle to describe. Theo wants them to leave thinking “I want that.”

The purpose is to make the opportunity tangible, invite corrections and earn sponsorship for discovery. Enthusiasm is not validated ROI, technical readiness or a deployment commitment. The actionable outcome is agreement on participants and timing for the next working session, using the [locked executive close](discovery-to-pilot.md#executive-close---locked-october-20).

A favorable authored case makes the intended experience clear. It does not establish that all cases should receive credit or that real cases always end neatly.

## What I would improve with more time

**Deepen evidence and realism before expanding scope.** Theo initially proposed adding more scope and accepted this refinement. Broaden only when discovery identifies another useful outcome.

Priorities:
1. Observe the work and validate the baseline and chosen case class with practitioners.
2. Prove actual integrations, case/action permissions and analyst usability.
3. Show counterexamples: Barbara requests further evidence, rejects or edits a recommendation, or lacks authority for a proposed action.
4. Exercise denied access, changed evidence, stale approval, failed/uncertain credit posting, reconciliation and duplicate prevention.
5. Evaluate the complete workflow and measure total active effort, quality and downstream rework/cost.

Retain existing Gate 0/build sequencing; this reflection does not imply checks or builds have run. Prioritize a reliable two-surface demonstration and truthful fallback over optional features.

## Understand how ChatGPT Enterprise got in the door

Theo wants more account context around the existing adoption. Apex's ChatGPT Enterprise footprint and original selection rationale remain fictional scenario assumptions.

In real discovery, establish:
- Why it was selected and what privacy, security, governance, safety, capability and commercial criteria mattered.
- Who sponsored/owns the deployment and what use cases employees actually adopted.
- What data, roles, retention, connected tools and actions were approved.
- Whether those approvals extend to Card Operations evidence, customer/API processing and financial actions; do not assume they do.
- What incumbent tools/native AI already do and where additional value is still needed.

Existing adoption gives a starting point for discovery and enablement, not blanket authorization for this new workflow. Keep [the customer-selection rationale](why-openai.md) distinct from personal career motivation.
